# Grade 4, continued (packets 58–72)

Packets 53–57 started grade 4 (place value, rounding, adding and subtracting big numbers, multiplying by one digit, remainders). The founder asked to keep building, a lot and well. This plan covers the rest of grade 4.

## Research

- **Eureka Math, grade 4 modules:** 1 place value, rounding, adding and subtracting; 2 metric unit conversions; 3 multi-digit multiplication and division (factors and multiples sit inside it); 4 angle measure and plane figures; 5 fraction equivalence, ordering and operations; 6 decimal fractions; 7 measurement with multiplication. Most time goes to whole-number operations (4.NBT) and fractions (4.NF).
- **Fractions in grade 4 (Common Core 4.NF):** equal fractions by multiplying top and bottom by the same number (4.NF.1), comparing with benchmarks or common denominators (4.NF.2), adding and subtracting like denominators, splitting a fraction into unit fractions, mixed numbers (4.NF.3), a fraction times a whole number, 5/4 = 5 × 1/4 (4.NF.4), then tenths and hundredths (4.NF.5–7). Unlike denominators wait for grade 5.
- **WeMath rules:** no number lines (decimals use hundred grids, strips and money; fraction sums use strips), help fades, plenty of no-help problems, "Remember?" review, challenge last. Measuring tools are allowed (the founder allowed rulers), so angles use a protractor.

Sources: [Eureka Math grade 4 overview](https://sites.google.com/centralvalleysd.net/nbarson/4th-grade-eureka-math), [EdReports: Eureka Math grade 4](https://edreports.org/reports/detail/eureka-math-2015/fourth-grade/gateway-one), [Grade 4 module overviews](https://www.greenhills.org/Page/2306), [TERC fraction progression](https://www.terc.edu/adultnumeracycenter/wp-content/uploads/sites/28/2025/10/Fraction-Progression.pdf), [Utah grade 4 core](https://schools.utah.gov/curr/mathematics/_mathematics_/_core/_major_works_tab_/CoreCourseOverviewGrade4.pdf), [Kansas grade 4 standards](https://www.ksde.gov/Portals/0/CSAS/Content%20Area%20%28A-E%29/Elementary%20K-6/4th%20Grade%20Math%20Standards.pdf).

## Order

Whole numbers first (they are needed for equal fractions and fraction × whole number), then fractions, then decimals (tenths and hundredths are fractions), then measurement and geometry.
