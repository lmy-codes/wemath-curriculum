# Grade 5, first round (packets 73–82)

The founder asked to keep building. Grade 4 is done (53–72), so this round starts grade 5.

## Research

Eureka Math grade 5 has six modules: 1 place value and decimal fractions; 2 multi-digit whole-number and decimal operations; 3 adding and subtracting fractions; 4 multiplying and dividing fractions and decimals; 5 volume and area; 6 the coordinate plane. Modules 1–5 are the main work of the grade (EdReports).

Sources: [Eureka Math grade 5 modules (school page)](https://sites.google.com/sunol.k12.ca.us/mrsfigueirasclass23-24/home/math), [EdReports: Eureka Math grade 5](https://edreports.org/reports/detail/eureka-math-2015/fifth-grade/gateway-one), [Great Minds: A Story of Units](https://greatminds.org/knowledge/resources/a-story-of-units).

## Order and packets

Modules 1, 2, 3 and part of 4 and 5. WeMath rules hold: no number lines (decimals use place-value charts, grids and money), help fades, a+b=c with the answer last, plenty of no-help problems, "Remember?" review, challenge last.

Later rounds: fractions as division and dividing with unit fractions (5.NF.3, 5.NF.7), expressions and order of operations (5.OA.1–2), number patterns (5.OA.3), line plots and conversions (5.MD.1–2), shapes (5.G.3–4). The coordinate plane (5.G.1–2) is built from two number lines, so it needs the founder's yes before it is planned.

Each packet: learning page, help that fades, many no-help problems by kind, "Remember?", word problems with the challenge last, a Check-In, and parts in new grade 5 assessments.
