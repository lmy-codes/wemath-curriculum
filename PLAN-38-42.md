## Research

- **Multiplying by tens (3.NBT.3).** Read 4 × 30 as "4 groups of 3 tens": that is 12 tens, which is 120. The
  standard asks for place value reasoning, not a "add a zero" rule. Watch 5 × 40: the fact 5 × 4 = 20 already ends
  in a zero, and 20 tens is 200. Sources: [CDE 3.NBT.3](https://www2.cde.ca.gov/cacs/id/web/343),
  [Varsity Tutors: multiply by multiples of 10](https://www.varsitytutors.com/practice/subjects/3rd-grade-math/help/multiply-by-multiples-of-10).
- **Area (3.MD.5–7).** Start by covering with unit squares and counting, then see that rows × columns gives the
  same number, then use side lengths with no squares drawn, then break a rectangle apart (a × (b + c) = a × b + a × c)
  and add the areas of two rectangles (L shapes). The usual mistake is mixing up area and perimeter. Sources:
  [Illustrative Mathematics 3.MD.C.7](https://tasks.illustrativemathematics.org/content-standards/3/MD/C/7),
  [CDE 3.MD.7c](https://www2.cde.ca.gov/cacs/id/web/360),
  [Connecticut grade 3 unit 5](https://test-cd.ct.gov/-/media/sde/math/units/gr3/gr_3_unit_5_10-2012.doc).
- **Perimeter (3.MD.8).** Walk around the edge and count the unit lengths, then add the side lengths, then find a
  missing side, then compare area and perimeter of the same rectangle.
- **Elapsed time (3.MD.1).** Tell time to the minute (count by 5s, then 1s). For intervals, jump to the next
  o'clock, then to the end, and add the jumps: the same route as packet 11 (jump to the next ten). Crossing the
  hour is the hard part, so it gets its own scaffolded parts. Sources:
  [Plainville grade 3 elapsed time](https://www.plainville.k12.ma.us/cms/lib/MA01000200/Centricity/Domain/253/Grade_3_Elapsed_Time.pdf),
  [Varsity Tutors: time intervals](https://www.varsitytutors.com/practice/subjects/3rd-grade-math/help/tell-time-and-calculate-intervals).
  Most programs use a number line here; we use packet 11's jump picture instead (a row of times with a curved
  arrow per jump), which is not a number line.
- **Two-step problems, all four operations (3.OA.8).** Bar models (packet 30) get equal pieces: a bar cut into
  4 equal pieces of 6 is 4 × 6; a whole of 30 cut into 5 equal pieces is 30 ÷ 5. Then two steps, then "does the
  answer make sense?" by rounding (3.OA.8 asks for estimation and rounding). Letters for unknowns are left out
  (a box is the unknown, as in every packet so far).

## Founder rules applied

- a + b = c order everywhere, the blank in the answer spot except where a missing number is the skill
  (missing side length in 39 and 40).
- No number lines. Elapsed time uses jumps between times, like packet 11.
- No question twice: every key checked with `check_questions.py`.
- Each packet: learning page, help fading (pictures → labels → boxes → no help, organized by kind of question),
  "Remember?" spaced review, word problems with the challenge last, a Check-In and parts in the grade 3 assessments.
