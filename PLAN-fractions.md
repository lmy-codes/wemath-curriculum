# Fractions: six packets instead of one

The founder said the old Fractions packet (36) was far too fast: it named, shaded, compared and found equal fractions in a couple of pages. They asked for several packets, one concept each, with many more practice problems, and for fractions of a group to get its own packet.

## Research

- **Common Core progression (Number and Operations, Fractions, 3–5).** Grade 3 starts with **unit fractions**: 1/b is one part when a whole is cut into b equal parts. Then a/b is **built from unit fractions**: 3/4 is three 1/4 pieces put together. Fractions bigger than 1 need no separate treatment at first; 5/3 is just five thirds. Wholes that are **collections of objects** (fractions of a group) come later, in grade 4 (4.NF.4).
- **Eureka Math, grade 3 Module 5 ("Fractions as Numbers").** The order is: equal parts → unit fractions, and building fractions greater than one from unit fractions → comparing unit fractions and specifying the whole → fractions on a number line (we skip this; strips instead) → equivalent fractions → comparing fractions. Denominators are 2, 3, 4, 6 and 8. The module comes **after Area** (Module 4), because 3.G.2 links the two: cut a shape into parts with equal area, and each part is a unit fraction of the whole.
- **Fractions of a group** is division first: 1/3 of 12 means sharing 12 into 3 equal groups (12 ÷ 3 = 4), and 2/3 of 12 is two of those groups. So it needs Division (35), and it fits best after the fraction-as-a-number packets.
- WeMath rules still hold: no number lines (fraction strips and the fraction wall instead), help fades, plenty of no-help problems, a "Remember?" review, and the challenge goes last.

Sources: [Progressions for the Common Core, Number and Operations – Fractions 3–5](https://www.isbe.net/Documents/number-fractions-3-5.pdf), [Eureka Math grade 3 Module 5 teacher edition](https://www.u-46.org/cms/lib/IL01804616/Centricity/Domain/5426/Grade3Module5Teacher.pdf), [Eureka Math² grade 3 Module 5 overview](https://greatminds.org/hubfs/Assets/Eureka%20Math%20Squared/EM2CA%20California%20Edition/2025%20Edition%20Files/topic-by-topic/Grade%203/EM2CA180_25A_TEG3_M5_Module%20Overview.pdf), [Fishtank: non-unit fractions are built from unit fractions](https://fishtanklearning.org/curriculum/math/3rd-grade/fractions/lesson-3).

## The six packets

Denominators 2, 3, 4, 6 and 8 (grade 3), plus 5 and 10 for more practice and as a head start on tenths in grade 4.

1. **39 Unit Fractions** (3.NF.1, 3.G.2). Equal parts (yes/no, now with 5, 6 and 8 parts), name the unit fraction from a picture, shade one part, find the whole from one piece, which piece is bigger (more parts, smaller pieces). No help: "1/8 is 1 of ☐ equal parts", "☐ pieces of 1/6 make 1 whole", "one fifth = 1/☐", bigger piece.
2. **40 Counting Fractions** (3.NF.1). 3/4 is three 1/4 pieces. Count the pieces, name and shade any fraction, count by fractions (1/5, 2/5, ☐, ☐), what's not shaded. No help both ways ("3 pieces of 1/8 = ☐/8", "5/6 = ☐ pieces of 1/6") and in words.
3. **41 Fractions and Wholes** (3.NF.3c). 4/4 = 1, fractions bigger than 1 (7/4 = 1 whole and 3/4), whole numbers as fractions (8/4 = 2, 3/1 = 3), less than, equal to or more than 1.
4. **42 Equal Fractions** (3.NF.3a–b). The fraction wall, strips lined up, shade to match, then no-help equal fractions (1/2 = ☐/8, 6/8 = 3/☐, chains like 1/2 = ☐/4 = ☐/8).
5. **43 Comparing Fractions** (3.NF.3d). Same bottom number, same top number, unit fractions, equal ones, and why both fractions must be of the same whole. Write <, > or =.
6. **44 Fractions of a Group** (bridge to 4.NF.4). 1/3 of 12 by sharing into groups (pictures with rings), then 2/3 of 12 is two groups; ring the groups yourself; "12 ÷ 3 = ☐, so 2/3 of 12 = ☐"; no help; word problems.

Each packet: learning page, help that fades, many no-help problems sorted by kind of question, "Remember?", word problems with the challenge last, and a Check-In. Each gets parts in the grade 3 assessments in place of the old fraction parts.
