# Grade 6 (packets 91 and up)

The founder agreed this order: ratios and rates, dividing fractions, multi-digit decimals and division, GCF and LCM, negative numbers (no number lines), expressions and equations, area of shapes, volume with fractions, surface area, statistics.

Every packet follows the newer rules: a lot of practice (80–110 problems), no more than 12 problems on a page, help that fades, "Remember?" review, word problems with the challenge last, a Check-In, and parts in the grade 6 assessments.
